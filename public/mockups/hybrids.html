A. Seed Shop

Fonts: Shrikhand (signage, packet names) + Pixelify Sans (HUD, receipt, dialog options) + Mali (body)

  • Awning red #a33224
  • Packet cream #fbf1d9
  • Shop plank #6b4a32
  • Panel #fff4d6
  • Leaf #3f6b35
  • The site is the shop from a farm game: the hero is a shopkeeper's dialog with choices to pick from, and the seed packets sit on wooden shelves as goods you can buy.
  • Results arrive as a torn receipt (counts plus what to buy) next to the planted tile plot, so the shopping list and the layout are one moment.
  • Step 3 of the planner is the shelf itself: you pick packets to add to your basket. Swapping a plant means grabbing a different packet.

B. Packet Quests

Fonts: Shrikhand (display) + Gaegu (body) + Pixelify Sans (numbers and HUD only)

  • Kraft #e9d6b0
  • Packet #fbf1d9
  • Packet red #a33224
  • Mustard #e3a72f
  • Ink #2b2118
  • It keeps #7's game layout (status bar, quest log, plot, inventory, weather), but every panel is printed as a seed envelope with a colored flap, zigzag bottom edge and an offset red shadow like misregistered print.
  • The type is packet-first: Shrikhand and Gaegu do the talking, and pixel type appears only for numbers and status readouts, so it reads as cozy print with a game layer.
  • Planner steps are envelopes too; a finished step's flap turns green, and the categories you pick become packets in your inventory.

C. Collector's Album

Fonts: Shrikhand (card names, headings) + Mali (body) + Pixelify Sans (rarity, stats, levels)

  • Binder green #2f4a3a
  • Album page #f3ead2
  • Classic red #a33224
  • Easy blue #2d6c8a
  • Level gold #e3a72f
  • A ring binder where each plant is a collectible trading card: a rarity tag that means difficulty, stats for spacing, harvest and sun, and Planted/Growing/Harvested stamps.

  • Planner steps are album pages with empty sleeves; finishing the form "deals" the cards that fit your space.

D. Pixel Packet Print

Fonts: Silkscreen (pixel display) + Pixelify Sans (UI) + Mali (body)

  • Walnut #3b2a20
  • Panel #fff4d6
  • Packet red #a33224
  • Sun gold #ffd166
  • Leaf #3f6b35
  • The seed packet is drawn in pixel art: the hero is a giant pixel packet with a stepped red flap and a zigzag edge, set on a dark walnut background like a game title screen.
  • The top-down pixel plot is split into patches, each labelled by a tiny packet tag (flap color, sprite, count, what to buy), all inside chunky beveled game panels.
  • The planner is three beveled step panels, with the sun compass as four pixel side tiles around the plot (two neighbors make a corner, like Southwest) and categories picked as mini packet tags.
