Plant sprites

Twenty original 16 × 24 pixel crops. Each grows from seed to harvest, then sways in the breeze. Click a packet to replay its growth.

Reduced motion is on, so plants are shown fully grown without animation.

Sheets live in /sprites/sheets/<id>.png: five 16 × 24 frames left to right (seed, sprout, young, mature, sway). Frame order and sizes are in /sprites/sheets/manifest.json.
